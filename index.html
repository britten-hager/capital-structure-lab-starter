Campus Deli and Sub Shop Question d

Case method: buy back at today's

Questions d(3) and d(4). Shares repurchased = D / P₀. EPS = net income / shares remaining. Price = EPS / rs. WACC uses book weights (the AGE solution's method for d(7)).

New-price method: buy back at the price after the announcement

Value of equity S = net income / rs. Value of the firm V = S + D. Price = V / shares before the buyback. Shares repurchased = D / price. WACC uses market weights.
